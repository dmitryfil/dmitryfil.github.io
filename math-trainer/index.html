Math Trainer (KD)

Small sessions. Stronger skills.

Practice arithmetic, fractions, decimals, patterns, money, time, and math reasoning at your own pace. Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard, try a daily challenge, and follow your progress on your device.

Free and unlimited

The planned public iOS release includes all exercises and local statistics, with no subscriptions, purchases, or practice limits. It has no ads and requires no online account.

Made for short practice sessions

  • Practice on iPhone or iPad, including offline.
  • Choose English, French, Italian, or Spanish in the app.
  • Track practice streaks and monthly quest progress, with optional Home Screen widgets.

The public iOS release is being prepared. For help with an existing test build, visit Support.
