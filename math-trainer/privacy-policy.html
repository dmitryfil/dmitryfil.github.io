Privacy Policy for Math Trainer (KD)

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how data is handled in Math Trainer for iPhone and iPad. The public iOS release is in preparation.

Summary

  • Practice history and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • When analytics is enabled, limited usage and practice data is sent to PostHog to help improve the app.
  • You can turn analytics off in the app without losing progress or access to features.
  • No names, email addresses, question text, raw answers, advertising identifiers, or recordings are included in app analytics.
  • The app has no ads or purchases. We do not sell personal information.

1. Data We Process

1.1 Local practice and settings

The app stores language and difficulty preferences, a local profile, and up to 500 recent practice sessions. Session records may include exercise text, selected and correct answers, accuracy, dates, and time spent. This supports statistics, streaks, and monthly quest progress.

On iOS, streak and quest summaries and the chosen language are shared locally with the app's Home Screen widget. Full local practice records are not uploaded to us. There is no online app account or app-provided cloud sync. Apple may include app data in device backups depending on your settings.

1.2 Optional usage analytics

When analytics is enabled, PostHog receives limited events about app visits, practice starts and completions, exercise categories, difficulty, language, hint use, correctness, attempts, and time spent. Events include a random installation ID, random visit/practice/question IDs, app version, and platform.

The installation ID helps measure return visits and usage over time. It is a pseudonymous identifier, not a guarantee of anonymity. Analytics excludes names, email addresses, device names, advertising IDs, exact question text, raw submitted answers, screen recordings, and session replay.

IP-based location enrichment is disabled. Network requests necessarily expose the connecting IP address to the receiving service. Analytics is subject to the choice and notice presented in the app; some builds disable it entirely.

1.3 Support requests

If you submit an issue through GitHub, the issue, your GitHub username, and any information or attachments you provide are public. We use reports to answer questions, investigate bugs, and improve the app. Please do not include personal information, children's identifying details, or analytics IDs.

2. Why Data Is Processed

Local data provides progress and statistics on your device. Optional analytics helps us understand which exercises are used, where learners struggle, whether sessions are completed, and whether people return to practice. Support information helps us investigate reported problems. Analytics is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Sharing and Service Providers

When enabled, analytics is processed by PostHog in the United States. See PostHog's Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal information.

Support and these web pages use GitHub. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its services. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies.

Apple's App Store, TestFlight, device diagnostics, and backup services operate under Apple's settings and policies.

4. Data Retention

Local practice history is limited to the 500 most recent sessions. Preferences and history remain on the device until removed or replaced. The analytics send queue is held in memory; turning analytics off clears unsent events and stops subsequent collection. Requests already in flight cannot be recalled.

Turning analytics off does not automatically delete events already received by PostHog. To request deletion, use the privacy contact process below. Public GitHub issues may remain available as part of the support history; GitHub controls its own service records under its policies.

5. Your Choices

Use the app's Privacy & analytics setting to turn analytics off without affecting progress or features. A new random analytics ID is created when analytics is re-enabled. If requesting deletion of analytics already shared, save the analytics ID displayed in the app before leaving the opt-out screen; do not post it publicly.

Delete the app using iOS Delete App to remove its local data; offloading preserves it. Manage device backups separately through Apple's settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The app can be used without providing a child's name, email address, date of birth, or online account. Analytics may include practice activity associated with a random installation ID when enabled; it is not inherently anonymous. Parents and guardians can disable analytics without restricting practice and should handle support requests on a child's behalf. Where required, parental authorization is needed before collecting children's personal data.

If you believe a child has shared personal information, use the privacy contact process below without posting that information publicly.

7. International Processing and Requests

PostHog and GitHub may process information outside your country, including in the United States. For questions about data, access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights, use the contact process below. Depending on your location, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised Last updated date. We will provide additional notice or obtain authorization where required before materially changing data practices.

9. Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/dmitryfil/kd-games-portal/issues.

For requests involving personal data, ask for a private contact method first. Do not include personal details, children's information, or your analytics ID in a public issue.
