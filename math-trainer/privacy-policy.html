Privacy Policy

Math Trainer (KD) · Public iOS release 1.0.0
Last updated: October 21, 2026

Math Trainer (KD) is developed by Dmitry Filippov. This policy describes the public iPhone and iPad release being prepared for the App Store. Contact: dimfilippov@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • No online account, advertising, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
  • Practice history and preferences stay on your device; the app does not upload them to the developer.
  • Usage analytics are disabled in the public release.
  • If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves language and difficulty preferences, a local profile creation date, and practice results. Results can include exercise text, selected and correct answers, accuracy, session dates, difficulty, and time spent. This supports local statistics, daily challenge streaks, and monthly quest progress.

The app keeps up to 500 recent practice sessions. On iOS, streak and quest summaries and your chosen language may also be shared locally with the app's Home Screen widget. They are not sent to the developer.

We do not operate a server account or cloud-sync service for this information. Depending on your Apple settings, app data may be included in device backups managed by Apple.

2. Analytics and tracking

The public release does not send usage events to PostHog. Its analytics client is not activated. The app does not use advertising identifiers, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Separately distributed internal test builds can offer analytics under a notice shown in the test app. That test configuration is not the public release covered here. Apple's App Store, TestFlight, and device diagnostic services operate under Apple's own settings and policies.

3. Support email

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to answer your request, investigate an issue, and maintain necessary support records. For EEA and UK users, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to requests and supporting the app, subject to applicable law.

Support email is handled through Gmail and your email provider. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell personal information. We retain correspondence only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request or meet applicable legal obligations; you can ask us to delete it.

4. Children and families

Children can use the public app without providing a name, email address, date of birth, or online account. Their practice history stays on the device. Parents and guardians should contact support on their behalf and avoid sending identifying information about a child.

If you believe personal information about a child has been sent to us, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

5. This website and service providers

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the website under its Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or embedded third-party content.

Support email and website hosting providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their applicable privacy policies and safeguards govern their processing. Google describes its practices in its Privacy Policy.

6. Your choices and requests

We cannot access or retrieve your on-device practice history. Delete the app using the iOS Delete App option to remove its local data; offloading preserves it. Manage existing device backups separately in Apple's settings.

For personal information you have sent by email, you may request access, correction, or deletion, or object to processing where applicable, by contacting dimfilippov@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restriction or portability and to complain to your local data protection authority. We may need to verify a request and retain information where legally required.

7. Policy changes

Changes will appear on this page with an updated date. We will update the policy before introducing materially different data practices, such as public usage analytics or online accounts, and provide notice or obtain consent where required.
