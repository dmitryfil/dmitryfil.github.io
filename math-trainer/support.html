Reporting an Issue

Include the app version, device model, iOS version, what happened, and what you expected. For an exercise problem, include the language, difficulty, exercise type, and steps to reproduce it. Use the math-trainer label.

GitHub issues and screenshots are public. Remove private information before posting. Parents and guardians should report issues on a child's behalf without including the child's name, school, date of birth, or other identifying details.

Common Questions

Is the app free?

The planned public release is free and unlimited, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or ads. No online account is needed to practice.

Does it work offline?

Exercises and local statistics work offline. Opening web pages or using GitHub support requires an internet connection and GitHub requires an account to submit an issue.

Where is my progress?

Practice history is stored on the device. Statistics are shown for the selected difficulty, so check your level if results appear to be missing. There is no app account or app-provided cloud sync.

Why is my widget out of date?

Open the app and complete a practice session. iOS controls when Home Screen widgets refresh, so changes may take time to appear.

How do I remove local data?

Use Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) → Math Trainer → Delete App. Offloading keeps the app's documents and data. Device backups are managed separately in Apple's settings. Removing local data does not delete previously submitted analytics.

Privacy Questions

Read the Privacy Policy. For a privacy request, open a general issue asking for a private contact method. Do not post personal data or your analytics ID publicly.
