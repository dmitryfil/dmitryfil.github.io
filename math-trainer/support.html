Please include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app language, difficulty, and the exercise involved. Tell us what happened and what you expected. A screenshot can help, but remove personal information before sending it.

Common questions

Do I need an account or subscription?

The public release is designed to be free and unlimited. No online account, subscription, or purchase is required.

Can I practice offline?

Exercises and local statistics work offline. Sending a support email or opening these web pages requires an internet connection.

Where is my progress?

Practice history is saved on the device. Statistics are shown for the selected difficulty, so check your level if results appear to be missing. The app does not provide an online account or its own cloud sync.

Why does a widget look out of date?

Open the app and complete a practice session. iOS controls when Home Screen widgets refresh, so changes may take time to appear.

How do I remove my local data?

On iPhone or iPad, use Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) → Math Trainer → Delete App. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data. Device backups are managed separately through Apple's settings.
